Infrastructure investments delivering results

Product Updates

Q4 delivered three major product releases including the highly anticipated AI-powered analytics dashboard, which achieved 78% adoption among enterprise customers within 60 days. Platform uptime improved to 99.97%, exceeding our 99.9% SLA commitment.

Team Growth

Headcount increased by 45 positions in Q4, with strategic hires in engineering (24), sales (12), and customer success (9). Employee satisfaction scores remained strong at 4.2/5.0.

Infrastructure

Completed migration to multi-region cloud architecture, reducing latency by 40% for APAC customers. Security certifications expanded to include ISO 27001 and SOC 2 Type II.
