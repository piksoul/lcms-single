The Quantum Computing Breakthrough That Changes Everything

Researchers at MIT have achieved a major milestone in quantum error correction, bringing practical quantum computers one step closer to reality. This breakthrough could revolutionize drug discovery, cryptography, and artificial intelligence within the next decade.

The new error correction technique reduces quantum bit error rates by 99.5%, solving one of the fundamental challenges that has prevented quantum computers from scaling. "This is the moment we've been working toward for 30 years," says Dr. Elena Rodriguez, lead researcher on the project.
